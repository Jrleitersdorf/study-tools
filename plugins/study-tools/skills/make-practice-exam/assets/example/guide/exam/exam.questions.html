Coffee and tea are substitutes. A frost in Brazil raises the price of coffee. Which of the following happens in the market for tea?

  1. A movement down along the demand curve for tea
  2. The demand curve for tea shifts to the right
  3. The supply curve for tea shifts to the right
  4. The demand curve for tea shifts to the left

A higher price of a substitute makes tea more attractive at every price of tea, so the whole demand curve for tea shifts right.

A drought destroys a third of this year's corn crop, and demand for corn does not change. What happens to the equilibrium price and quantity of corn?

  1. Price falls, quantity rises
  2. Price falls, quantity falls
  3. Price rises, quantity rises
  4. Price rises, quantity falls

Supply shifts left along an unchanged demand curve: the price rises and the quantity falls.

A city's transit agency estimates that the price elasticity of demand for bus rides is 0.4 in absolute value. Which of the following is true?

  1. Demand is elastic, so raising the fare lowers total revenue
  2. Demand is unit elastic, so raising the fare leaves total revenue unchanged
  3. Demand is inelastic, so raising the fare raises total revenue
  4. Demand is inelastic, so raising the fare lowers total revenue

An elasticity below 1 is inelastic: quantity falls by a smaller percentage than the price rises, so revenue rises.

The equilibrium price of gasoline is $4.00 a gallon. The government sets a maximum price of $3.00. Which of the following results?

  1. A shortage of gasoline
  2. A surplus of gasoline
  3. No change, because the maximum is below the equilibrium price
  4. An increase in the quantity of gasoline supplied

A price ceiling below equilibrium binds: quantity demanded rises and quantity supplied falls, so there is a shortage.

In one hour, Ana can bake 6 pies or sew 4 shirts, and Ben can bake 2 pies or sew 2 shirts. Which of the following is true?

  1. Ana has a comparative advantage in both goods, so they cannot gain from trade
  2. Ben has a comparative advantage in pies and Ana in shirts
  3. Ana has a comparative advantage in pies and Ben in shirts
  4. Neither has a comparative advantage, because Ana has an absolute advantage in both goods

A pie costs Ana \(4/6 \approx 0.67\) shirts and Ben 1 shirt, so Ana has the lower opportunity cost in pies and Ben in shirts. Absolute advantage in both goods does not rule out gains from trade.

Demand for insulin is perfectly inelastic. The government places a $10 tax per vial on sellers. Who bears the tax?

  1. Buyers pay the entire tax
  2. Sellers pay the entire tax, because the law puts it on them
  3. Buyers and sellers each pay $5
  4. Nobody, because the quantity sold does not change

With perfectly inelastic demand, buyers purchase the same quantity at any price, so the price they pay rises by the full $10. Who legally pays does not decide who bears the tax.

Which of the following is consumer surplus?

  1. The price of a good minus the seller's cost of producing it
  2. The quantity of a good left unsold at the market price
  3. Total revenue minus total cost
  4. What buyers are willing to pay for a good minus what they actually pay

Consumer surplus is willingness to pay minus the price paid, summed over buyers. (a) is producer surplus per unit, (b) is a surplus of goods, and (c) is profit.

When household incomes rise by 10%, the quantity of bus rides demanded falls by 5%. Which of the following describes bus rides?

  1. A normal good with an income elasticity of 0.5
  2. An inferior good with an income elasticity of \(-0.5\)
  3. An inferior good with an income elasticity of \(-2\)
  4. A luxury good with an income elasticity of 2

Income elasticity is \(-5\%/10\% = -0.5\); a negative income elasticity means an inferior good.

In the market for concert tickets, demand is \(Q_d = 100 - 2P\) and supply is \(Q_s = 20 + 2P\), with \(P\) in dollars. What is consumer surplus at the equilibrium?

Equilibrium at \(P = 20\), \(Q = 60\); demand reaches zero at \(P = 50\), so consumer surplus is \(\tfrac12 \cdot 60 \cdot 30 = 900\).

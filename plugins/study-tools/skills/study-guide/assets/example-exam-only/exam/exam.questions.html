An isotope has a half-life of 6 hours. What fraction of a sample is left after one day?

  1. 1/4
  2. 1/8
  3. 1/16
  4. 1/24

One day is \(24/6=4\) half-lives, so \((1/2)^4=1/16\) is left.

A sample has an activity of 800 decays per second. Its half-life is 3 days. What is its activity 6 days later?

Two half-lives: \(800/4=200\) decays per second.

Carbon-14 has a half-life of 5730 years. A sample with half the carbon-14 of living tissue is 2865 years old.

False. Half the carbon-14 is one half-life: 5730 years.

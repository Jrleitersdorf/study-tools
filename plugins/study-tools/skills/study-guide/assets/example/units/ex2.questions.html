Carbon-14 has a half-life of 5730 years. A bone contains 1/8 of the carbon-14 found in living bone. How old is it?

\(1/8=(1/2)^3\): three half-lives, \(3\times5730=17190\) years.

In \(t=\frac{t_{1/2}}{\ln 2}\ln\frac{N_0}{N}\), what does \(N_0\) stand for in radiocarbon dating?

  1. The carbon-14 level in living tissue
  2. The carbon-14 left in the sample today
  3. The number of half-lives elapsed

\(N_0\) is the starting level, the one the organism kept while alive.

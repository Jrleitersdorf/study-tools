Practice exam

Seven questions worth 10 points, in the format of the real quiz: multiple choice, true or false, short numeric answers and one short piece of code, with no calculator. They cover the two lectures in this guide, about two fifths of the quiz, so they take 20 minutes rather than the full 50. Work through them in one sitting and press Submit exam at the end; nothing is graded before that, and you mark the code answer yourself against its rubric.

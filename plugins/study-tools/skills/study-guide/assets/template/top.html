    This is a practice exam in the format of {{exam_name}}. Open it below, start the timer, answer every question as you would on the day, and press Submit exam at the end. Nothing is graded before that.

    How this practice exam works

    After you submit, each question shows whether you got it right, and the score comes with a breakdown by topic, weakest first. Show answer reveals the key with a worked solution. Written answers show a model answer and a rubric instead: compare your answer with them and mark it yourself as Full marks, Partly or Missed it, and the score updates.

    Solve it for me has Claude work a question through step by step, and Get feedback has Claude read a written answer against the rubric and suggest a mark (the mark stays yours). The Ask button at the bottom right answers questions about the exam; before you submit it gives hints only. Your answers save as you go.

    Each {{unit_lower}} is its own page. Pick one below or in the contents, read it to the end, answer its questions, rate your confidence, and move on with the Next button at the bottom.

    How this guide works

    Each {{unit_lower}} is written as one continuous explanation that builds the ideas in the order that makes them easiest to understand, with the equations where they are needed and what each part means. The small label under each section heading names the source material it covers.

    Amber boxes are pitfalls: traps, sign conventions and common confusions. An {{exam_hint_label}} tag marks something the instructor said or signaled will be tested; {{skip_label}} marks what they said will not be asked.

    Each {{unit_lower}} ends with practice questions. Check tells you only whether you are right; Show answer reveals the answer with a worked solution. Written questions show a model answer and a rubric when you compare, and you mark your own answer. Solve it for me has Claude work the question through on paper, step by step (it counts as wrong). The Ask button at the bottom right saves a question about the section you are reading and answers it in place.

    Start here

    Each {{unit_lower}} is its own page. Pick one below or in the contents, read it to the end, answer its questions, rate your confidence, and move on with the Next button at the bottom.

    How this guide works

    Each {{unit_lower}} is written as one continuous explanation that builds the ideas in the order that makes them easiest to understand, with the equations where they are needed and what each part means. The small label under each section heading names the source material it covers.

    Amber boxes are pitfalls: traps, sign conventions and common confusions. An {{exam_hint_label}} tag marks something the instructor said or signaled will be tested; {{skip_label}} marks what they said will not be asked.

    Each {{unit_lower}} ends with practice questions. Check tells you only whether you are right; Show answer reveals the answer with a worked solution, and Solve it for me has Claude work the question through on paper, step by step (it counts as wrong). The Ask button at the bottom right saves a question about the section you are reading and answers it in place.

    The practice exam mixes questions from every {{unit_lower}} in the format of {{exam_name}}. Nothing is graded until you submit it, so take it in one sitting, ideally after working through the {{unit_lower_plural}}.
